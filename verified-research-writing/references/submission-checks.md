# Submission and assignment compliance checklist

Read the user's rubric, journal author instructions, institutional template, or lab manual when supplied. Extract requirements into a short checklist before drafting; do not assume that a generic article structure satisfies a specific venue.

## Requirements to capture

- Document type, required section order/headings, and any required structured abstract.
- Word/page limits, language, formatting/template, citation style, and reference-list rules.
- Figure/table count, file/format requirements, captions, numbering, units, and in-text callouts.
- Required methods/reporting checklist or registration details.
- Required declarations: ethics approval/consent (if applicable), funding, conflicts, data/material/code availability, author contributions, and AI-use disclosure when the institution or venue requires it.
- Any explicit rubric criteria, marking weights, learning outcomes, or grading constraints.

## Final consistency pass

Check each item and label it **met**, **not met**, **not applicable**, or **cannot verify**:

1. Every rubric/venue requirement is mapped to a manuscript location or flagged as unmet.
2. Section structure, length, style, abstract, keywords, and headings follow the supplied rules.
3. In-text citations and reference-list entries match both ways; bibliographic details and identifiers were checked.
4. Every factual and numerical claim has evidence in the source ledger or supplied data. Full-text/abstract/metadata-only status is represented accurately.
5. Results, tables, figures, captions, units, and text agree with the actual source data. Calculated values can be reproduced.
6. Methods describe only the protocol and procedures actually supplied or verified.
7. Required disclosures are present only when supported by user-provided information. Never fabricate approval numbers, consent, registration, funding, conflicts, author roles, or data availability.
8. Any required reporting checklist is selected for the actual design, completed against the draft, and not claimed as satisfied if items remain unchecked.
9. Unresolved requirements and missing facts are visible to the user before submission.

Do not certify acceptance, publication readiness, plagiarism clearance, or compliance with a requirement that could not be inspected. A checklist supports review; it is not a substitute for the journal's current instructions or the author's final approval.
